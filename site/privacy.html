Fleet Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Fleet Studio is a private tool used by its owner, and by the family and friends the owner invites, to produce videos and publish them to their own YouTube channels.

What data we use

  • YouTube access (Google OAuth). When you connect a channel, Google gives the tool a token. It allows the tool to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic channel and video information (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • Files you add. Audio files, avatar images, thumbnails and the videos the tool makes from them.
  • Settings. Channel names, schedules and the production queue.

How it is used

The data is used only to make videos and upload them to the YouTube channel you connected, at the time you schedule. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising. We do not read your comments, messages or analytics.

Where it is stored

YouTube tokens and API keys are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository and are used only by its automated production runs. Login profiles are encrypted in your browser. Uploaded audio files are deleted after the video is made. The finished video files are kept for 7 days, and only so you can download them.

Google API Services

Fleet Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Fleet Studio uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or Google security settings. After that, the stored token stops working.
